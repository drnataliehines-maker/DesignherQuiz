The 8 Season Archetypes

Your archetype reflects the season you're in right now — the work you're being called to do, the energy you're being asked to trust.

🗝️ The Sovereign 🏛️ The Architect 🌱 The Healer 🪞 The Truth-Teller 🎨 The Creator 🌟 The Light-Bearer 🤝 The Connector 🦋 The Pathfinder
🗝️
The Sovereign
You are in a season of radical self-ownership.
This is your year to stop seeking permission. You are reclaiming authority over your own life — your choices, your energy, your direction. Nothing gets to take up space that you didn't consciously invite in.
Core Values
Sovereignty Power Boundaries Freedom Boldness Courage Self-Worth
🏛️
The Architect
You are in a season of intentional building.
You are not just dreaming — you are constructing. This season is about laying foundations that last, executing with precision, and turning vision into something you can hold. You are building your life on purpose.
Core Values
Vision Ambition Excellence Discipline Leadership Legacy Purpose
🌱
The Healer
You are in a season of restoration.
Something in you is mending — and you are making space for it. This isn't weakness; it's the most courageous kind of work. You are choosing wholeness over hustle, integration over performance.
Core Values
Healing Vulnerability Grace Presence Balance Gratitude Faith
🪞
The Truth-Teller
You are in a season of radical honesty.
You are done performing. The stories you've been telling yourself — and the ones others have told about you — are being examined and released. What remains is truer, and lighter, than anything that came before.
Core Values
Truth Authenticity Wisdom Clarity Presence Boundaries Self-Worth
🎨
The Creator
You are in a season of becoming.
Something new is emerging through you — and you are brave enough to let it. This season is defined by experimentation, expression, and the thrill of making something that didn't exist before you touched it.
Core Values
Creativity Reinvention Boldness Purpose Freedom Joy Abundance
🌟
The Light-Bearer
You are in a season of radiant overflow.
You have tended to yourself long enough that you are now spilling over. Joy is not something you earn — it is something you embody. This season, your presence alone is a gift to every room you enter.
Core Values
Joy Radiance Celebration Gratitude Abundance Generosity Presence
